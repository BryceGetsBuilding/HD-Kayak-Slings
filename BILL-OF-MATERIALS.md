# Bill of Materials — Kayak Cradle Pair

Everything needed to build **two** identical sling cradles for an Old Town
Sportsman 106 PDL (36" wide, ~107 lb, pedal drive removed). One trip to Home
Depot or Lowe's covers all of it. Quantities are **for the pair** unless a
"per cradle" column says otherwise.

See `cut-sheet.png` for the board-by-board cutting layout and `BUILD.md` /
`build-guide.pdf` for the step-by-step.

---

## A. Lumber

| # | Item | Spec | Buy | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 2×4 stud | 8 ft; SPF/DF stud grade | **7** | 6 are used (~16 pieces, ~42 bd-ft); the 7th covers a bad cut and gives offcuts for gussets + post-top pads. Use ground-contact **pressure-treated** or **cedar** if the cradles live outdoors. |

## B. Cut pieces — cut from the 2×4s (total for BOTH cradles)

All pieces are 2×4 (actual 1½" × 3½").

| # | Piece | Finished length | Qty (pair) | Per cradle | End cuts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Post | 30" | 4 | 2 | square both ends |
| B2 | Foot | 30" | 4 | 2 | square; round the top **outer** corners after assembly |
| B3 | Bottom rail | 36½" | 2 | 1 | square; laid flat; **cut to fit exactly between the two posts** |
| B4 | V-brace | ~25¾" on the long edge → **cut to fit** | 4 | 2 | **45° bevel at each end, perpendicular to each other** (a right trapezoid, not a parallelogram — flip the board end-for-end between cuts) |
| B5 | Foot stretcher *(optional)* | 39½" | 2 | 1 | square; laid flat across both feet |
| B6 | Brace gusset *(optional)* | ~7" | 4–6 | 2–3 | from 2×4 or ½" ply offcuts |
| B7 | Post-top pad *(optional)* | 3½" | 2 | 1 | scrap 2×4, or a carpet / rubber pad |

## C. Fasteners & hardware (total for BOTH cradles)

| # | Item | Spec | Used | Buy | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Carriage bolt | 5⁄16"-18 × 2½", zinc | 8 | **10** (+2 spare) | 2 side-by-side through each post top (4 posts) |
| C2 | Nylon-insert lock nut | 5⁄16"-18, zinc | 8 | **10** | one per carriage bolt, on the outside face |
| C3 | Fender washer | 5⁄16" ID, 1¼–1½" OD, zinc | 8 | **10** (+2) | **outside only, under each nut** (over the webbing) — spreads clamp load so the nut can't cut through it. No washer under the dome head — it seats flush against the wood on the inside. |
| C4 | Structural / deck screw | 3", #9–#10, exterior-rated | 28 | **1 lb box (~75)** | every wood joint — see the screw schedule below |
| C5 | Flat tie / nail plate | ~3" × 5" galvanized (e.g. Simpson TP35) | 0 or 4 | **optional** | over the webbing on each post top — cosmetic / extra abrasion protection only |

### Screw schedule (item C4)

| Joint | Per cradle | Pair | How it's driven |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foot → post | 4 (2 per foot) | 8 | up through the foot into the post; 1" either side of centre → 2" apart, ¾" edge distance |
| Rail end → foot | 4 (2 per end) | 8 | straight down through the rail into the foot; same 1"-off-centre layout (forms a rectangle with the foot→post screws) |
| Brace → post | 2 (1 per brace) | 4 | horizontally from the brace face into the post, ~1½" below the brace/post tip |
| Brace → rail | 2 (1 per brace) | 4 | down through the brace's bottom end into the rail (driven from the brace's top face) |
| Stretcher → foot *(optional)* | 2 (1 per foot) | 4 | down through the stretcher into each foot |
| **Total** | **14** | **28** | pre-drill a pilot for every screw within ~2" of an end |

## D. Sling / webbing

| # | Item | Spec | Qty | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Strap webbing | **2" or 3" polyester** tow / recovery / tree-saver strap | ~7 ft per cradle (~14 ft total) | One **2" × 20 ft** polyester tow strap (Husky, Home Depot #59925 — loop ends, no hooks) cut in half does both cradles. Cut the sewn loops / hooks off, cut to length, **melt-seal the raw ends** with a lighter. **Polyester, not nylon** — width is what matters, not the strap's rated strength. |

3" options if you want the wider strap: *Keeper 3" × 8 ft tree-saver* (buy 2) or
*VEVOR 3" × 20 ft* (buy 1, cut in half). By the yard: "Country Brook Design
3 inch polyester webbing", ~14 ft.

|---|---|
| 80-grit sandpaper or a rasp | ease the four foot ends, round the post tops where the strap bends |

## F. Tools (not consumed)

Miter saw (best for the repeated 45° V-brace cut) or a circular saw + speed
square · drill / driver · drill bits: 5⁄16", ¼", and a pilot bit · ½" socket
or wrench · tape measure · speed square · 2–3 clamps · safety glasses.

## Cost estimate — the pair

Rough 2026 US big-box pricing; varies by region. Treat as a ballpark.

| Group | Estimate |
|---|---|
| 7 × 8-ft 2×4 @ ~$4 | ~$28 |
| Carriage bolts + lock nuts + fender washers | ~$10 |
| 1 lb box of 3" deck screws | ~$9 |
| 2" × 20 ft polyester tow strap | ~$15 |
| **Total** | **~$55–75** |

Optional galvanized tie plates add ~$3 each (×4 = ~$12). Stainless hardware for
an outdoor build roughly doubles the fastener cost.
